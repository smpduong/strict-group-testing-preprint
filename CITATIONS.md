# Source attribution

- Damaschke, Sheikh Muhammad and Wiener (2014), DOI https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcta.2014.04.005. Section 8, Proposition 15: n <= binomial(m,2)+3 permits m+3 tests. Values m=7,8 give the quoted 24- and 31-item upper bounds. The paper also records t(9,2,2)=7 and t(10,2,2)=8, yielding f(7)=9. Table 1 reports the known one-stage capacity g(9)=12; this capacity is not needed for the current manuscript, whose nonadaptive endpoints follow directly from its pair-antichain lemma.
- Grankvist (2017), Searching for Search Strategies, https://odr.chalmers.se/server/api/core/bitstreams/0ce48183-ab4e-444b-8372-8da3b9d93907/content. Section 5.4 establishes t(14,2,2)=9 and t(20,2,2)=t(21,2,2)=10. Combined with the 13-item eight-test upper bound, this supplies f(8)=13.
